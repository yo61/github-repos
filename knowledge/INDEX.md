# Knowledge index

Routes to domain knowledge folders that span the managed repositories. Each domain holds
`hypotheses.md` (need more data) and, once a hypothesis is confirmed three or more times,
`rules.md` (apply by default). Repository-specific knowledge stays in that repository.

- [dependency-updates](dependency-updates/hypotheses.md) — open hypotheses about working through
  bot PRs and migrating linters across repositories. The jactionlint playbook is
  [`../docs/jactionlint-migration.md`](../docs/jactionlint-migration.md).
